## 🌟 The 5W / 1H Framework
- **WHO:** Citizens, marginalized communities, undertrials, pro-bono attorneys, and civic organizations.
- **WHAT:** An open-source, quantum-resistant artificial intelligence protocol for constitutional fidelity, democratic checks, and pro-poor legal upliftment.
- **WHEN:** 24/7 instantaneous rights advisory, emergency bail generation, and real-time pre-enactment legislative auditing.
- **WHERE:** Global Web4 multi-chain lattice, rural civic tele-law outposts, and mobile Reown AppKit QR portals.
- **WHY:** To ensure that supreme legal protections and judicial justice are universal rights, not luxury commodities for the wealthy.
- **HOW:** Synergistic deployment of NIST PQC lattice cryptography, ZK-SNARK privacy grievance circuits, X402 Bazaar pro-bono micro-escrows, and REA multi-agent consensus.
